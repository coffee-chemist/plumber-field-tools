Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Overview

Plumber Field Tools is designed to work without requiring an account or collecting personal information from users.

Data Collection

Plumber Field Tools does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data with the developer or with third parties.

Information Stored on Your Device

Information you enter into the app—such as job names, customer names, addresses or locations, notes, saved calculations, favorites, recent tools, and app settings—is stored locally on your device.

This locally stored information is used only to provide app functionality. The developer does not receive or access that information through the app.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current version of Plumber Field Tools does not include advertising, third-party analytics, user tracking, or cross-app tracking.

Accounts and Cloud Services

The current version does not require a user account and does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization or cloud storage.

Third-Party Services

The app may rely on operating-system and platform services necessary to run on iOS. Plumber Field Tools does not intentionally transmit user-entered job data or saved calculations to the developer or to advertising/analytics providers.

Children's Privacy

Plumber Field Tools is a professional utility app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If future versions add features that change the app's data practices, this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to caffeinateddevssupport@gmail.com.
